# Steering PCB

My first PCB project! I designed and assembled a steering PCB for an electric vehicle at UBC Supermileage using KiCAD. This incorporated essential components such as an emergency shutoff, starter motor, and driver control interfaces. Streamlined the prototype vehicle's electrical layout by soldering, crimping, and organizing dozens of components to facilitate quick assembly and disassembly in testing and maintenance.
